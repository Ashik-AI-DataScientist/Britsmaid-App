Open Britsmaid

Android

  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu beside the address bar.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install and create shortcut, then confirm Add or Install.
  4. Open the Britsmaid icon on your home screen and sign in.

The wording varies by phone and browser. If an Install Britsmaid button appears above, you can use that instead.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). In some Safari versions, open More first.
  3. Scroll to Add to Home Screen. Keep the name Britsmaid.
  4. If offered, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  5. Open the new Britsmaid icon and sign in.

A few things to know

Already inside WhatsApp or an email app? Use its menu to open the link in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone before adding the icon.

Contact your Britsmaid administrator to request an account and access based on your role, properties and duties.
