01 · Everyday access

Start here

Britsmaid is a private workspace for managing villas, stays, cleaning and owner accounts. Your administrator creates your account and chooses what you can see.

Open the app

  1. Open britsmaid-test.onrender.com.
  2. Use your assigned email and password.
  3. You will land in the admin dashboard, owner portal or cleaning workspace for your role.
  4. If a property or task is missing, ask the admin to check your access.

Internet is required. The hosted app works independently of the office PC and can be used on different Wi-Fi networks or mobile data.

Add the Britsmaid icon to your phone

Android: open the link in Chrome → menu → Add to Home screen or Install → confirm.

iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. If offered, keep Open as Web App enabled.

Open the new icon and sign in. Adding an icon does not create an account.

Open the installation guide ↗
Current app format: a branded home-screen web app. It is not published on the Play Store or App Store and does not provide full offline working. Browser menu wording varies by device.

03 · Access and privacy

Who can do what?

The four working roles
RoleAccessResponsibility
AdminAll properties and app data; accounts; supported overrides; security/storage controls.Set access, check operations and record exceptions.
OwnerOnly assigned properties: earnings, payouts, stays, reports and completed cleaning evidence.Review their property's performance and condition.
CleanerAssigned tasks within permitted properties; room evidence/checklists; own training.Document and clean, then submit for review.
SupervisorCleaning tasks, eligible cleaner assignment and review within assigned properties.Inspect, approve or request corrections.

Cleaners and supervisors do not receive guest-finance, owner-payout or guest-ID document access. Owners cannot read another owner's property by changing a link. Guest ID images are reserved for authorised admins once separate storage is activated.

Use individual accounts. The app link does not grant access. The admin must create a login and assign a role/properties. An app admin login does not automatically grant access to GitHub, Render, MongoDB or Cloudflare accounts.
04 · Daily work

Simple guides for each role

Admin · prepare the day and handle exceptions
  1. Home: review arrivals, departures, unpaid amounts and cleaning to verify.
  2. Villas: check the property, owner, rooms, cover and calendar links.
  3. Team & logins: assign each person's role and properties.
  4. Calendar: add/correct a booking or block unavailable dates.
  5. Cleaning: assign, inspect and approve or return work.
  6. Finance: check amounts, create invoices/statements and record actual payouts.
  7. Notices: investigate failures; check backups and photo settings.

Failed work after approval? Open the cleaning record → Admin: reopen, reassign or complete → choose the action/cleaner → add a reason → Save with note. Rework starts a fresh round; earlier evidence remains in history.

Supported overrides can complete cleaning despite missing photos, checklist or review. Guest workflows also have supported overrides with notes. This is not universal undo: permanent deletions, the 45-day minimum and storage protections remain enforced.

Owner · review earnings, payments and condition
  1. Sign in and open your property.
  2. Earnings: choose the month, review the settlement and download its statement PDF.
  3. Payouts: review recorded payments, references and receipts.
  4. Stays: review current/upcoming and recent bookings.
  5. Updates: open reports and completed cleaning records with before/after photos.
  6. Refresh after someone updates a record; ask the admin about a mismatch.

Expected earnings are not proof of payment. Compare payments and balance due. Ask the admin for a photo hold promptly if evidence is needed for a dispute or payout review.

Cleaner · photograph, clean, check and submit
  1. Open To do and your assigned property/task.
  2. Check the room name and take clear Before photos.
  3. Clean and set up; take After photos of the same areas.
  4. Complete the checklist: surfaces, linen, bathroom, supplies and damage check.
  5. Repeat for every room/common area and add useful notes.
  6. Wait for photos to save, then submit for review.
  7. Check for corrections. Needs review is not final approval.

Choose Take photo or Choose from library. If the browser opens a chooser instead of the camera, take the picture in the camera app and select it from the library.

For a locked/completed task, ask the supervisor/admin to return or reopen it. Follow pending-photo retry instructions and keep it attached to its original room/phase.

My training: read/watch your assigned lessons, practise, then acknowledge completion. Training does not block urgent cleaning tasks.

Supervisor · assign, inspect and review
  1. Open Review for submissions or Unassigned for tasks needing a cleaner.
  2. Select an active cleaner with access to that property. Ask the admin to correct access if necessary.
  3. Inspect every room's before/after photos, checklist and notes.
  4. Approve good work or return it with a clear correction note.
  5. Use To do and Done to follow progress.
  6. Ask the admin to reopen approved work or hold dispute evidence.

Your access stays within assigned properties. Staff do not need cloud-provider accounts for daily work.

05 · Feature directory

Features and where to find them

Open a topic for its purpose, location and a quick check that it worked.

Villas, owners, rooms and emergency contacts

Admin → Villas → property → Edit

Store villa name/location, owner and alternate contacts, emergency/other contacts, base nightly rate, owner/Britsmaid revenue split, electricity arrangement, notes and rooms/common areas. Choose a cover from the camera or library.

Why: bookings, cleaning and owner accounts need the correct property setup. Check: save, reopen and confirm the details/cover persisted. A villa record does not create its owner's login automatically.

Bookings and blocked dates

Admin → Home → New booking, or Calendar

Choose the property, dates, guest, source/OTA, status, amount and commission details where applicable. Edit stays or mark dates blocked when unavailable.

Why: maintain one operational availability record. Check: the correct property/dates, saved calendar entry and related cleaning task. Normal validation rejects invalid dates and overlapping active stays/blocks. A checkout date does not block that night's next booking.

Cleaning, checklists, review and readiness

Admin → Home → Cleaning; staff → cleaning workspace; owner → property → Updates

Capture room-by-room before/after photos, checklist, notes, assignment and supervisor review. Approved completion updates readiness. Another unfinished task can correctly keep the property marked as needing cleaning.

Why: make work and approval traceable. Check: saved photos, room/phase and task status. Reopening/reassignment keeps earlier evidence and admin actions in history.

Invoices and PDF sharing

Admin → Finance → Invoices, or Home → Invoice

Create guest-stay or client-service invoices with recipient details, line items, quantities/rates, discount, tax percentage, dates and notes. The app calculates totals, allocates a number, tracks paid/unpaid and offers Share PDF.

Why: issue and track consistent bills. Check: recipient, property/booking link, dates, amounts and PDF. The tax field calculates; it does not choose the correct tax treatment. Marking paid does not collect payment. Zoho sync is not connected.

Expenses, revenue splits and owner statements

Admin → property → Bills & deductions; Finance → Owners

Record property expenses and review monthly settlements: bookings, OTA commissions, applicable deductions, agreed shares and electricity treatment. Generate a statement PDF.

Why: explain the expected owner payout. Check: property, month, agreements, bookings and expenses before issuing the statement.

Payout records and receipts

Admin → owner statement/payout workflow; owner → property → Payouts

Record the amount, payment date, method and reference after an actual payment; download its receipt. Owners compare payments with expected payout and remaining balance.

Why: distinguish money due from money paid. Check: the bank/payment record agrees with the app. Recording a payout does not transfer money.

OTA performance and owner reports

Admin → Finance → OTA reports / Owners; Home → Owner report

Review channel revenue, bookings, nights, average daily rate and commission with villa breakdowns. Create property/month condition reports with notes, photos/videos and a settlement snapshot.

Why: explain performance and property condition. Check: property, month and intended attachments before sharing. Someone who has a report link can open that shared report: send it only to intended recipients. Report attachments are excluded from cleaning-photo expiry.

Cleaner training and refreshers

Admin → Home → Cleaner training; cleaner → My training

Create drafts, publish reviewed lessons, assign individual/all active cleaners, set due dates and optional onboarding/refresher intervals, and inspect pending/overdue/completed history. Reassignments preserve earlier records. Archiving stops new assignments.

Why: keep instructions consistent. Check: the published version and assigned cleaner. Publishing is separate from saving a draft. Completion is an acknowledgement, not a practical exam. Quiz/pass-mark gates are not included.

Team accounts, invitations and password resets

Admin → Team / Team & logins

Create individual admin, owner, cleaner or supervisor accounts; assign properties; edit permitted details; reset passwords; deactivate access; and prepare an invitation to share.

Why: people see the right work/data. Check: an owner sees assigned villas and a cleaner sees assigned tasks. New/reset passwords need at least 12 characters. Invitations are not automatically emailed by an external mail service.

Guest invitations, ID review and check-in

Admin → Guest IDs; invited guest → private check-in link

The workflow supports creating/replacing/revoking invitations, guest document submission, admin approval/rejection, review history and verified check-in. Supported overrides require an admin note.

Real-ID storage is not active. Separate private ID storage/encryption configuration is pending. Do not collect IDs through ordinary photo uploads or assume R2 media setup activated this feature. Automated Indian-ID authenticity checks are not connected.

Once activated: invite the right booking, have the guest submit the required slots, inspect documents privately and complete check-in. Invitations expire, and replaced/revoked links stop working. Normal check-in depends on booking dates and verification. Manual review does not guarantee that a document is genuine.

Service notices, security and backups

Admin → Notices → Security, storage & backups

Review service errors/recovery, check storage and the last verified backup, request a backup, enroll an authenticator and manage per-property cleaning-photo retention.

Why: spot problems and protect records. Check: backup timestamp, notices and intended property setting. The in-app panel cannot deliver an outage alert while the whole app is unavailable.

06 · Availability without confusion

Calendar and OTA syncing

Bring an OTA calendar into Britsmaid

  1. Open the villa's Edit page.
  2. Find OTA iCal links.
  3. Select the OTA and paste that villa's HTTPS export link from its OTA account.
  4. Add/save, then use Sync now and check the calendar.

Send availability to an OTA

  1. Find Send this villa's availability to an OTA on the villa's Edit page.
  2. Select the destination and create its iCal link.
  3. Paste it into the matching villa's Import calendar setting on the OTA.
  4. Test a booking/block and cancellation across both calendars.

Sources include Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip, Expedia, Vrbo, Other and Direct. A listed OTA still needs a usable feed from that platform. MakeMyTrip has source selection, reporting, colour and calendar handling; it is not a direct MakeMyTrip API connection.

Direct · greenAgoda · purpleMakeMyTrip · orangeCleaning due/review · yellow dotCleaning complete · white square, black border

Britsmaid checks imports about every 10 minutes while running. Free hosting can sleep and delay the check. Other OTAs decide when to fetch your export; Britsmaid cannot force an instant refresh. Sync now imports into Britsmaid rather than forcing an OTA to read Britsmaid.

Exports share availability, not guest names, identity images or financial amounts. Destination-specific feeds exclude that destination's own imported bookings to reduce loops. Keep export links for intended calendar services only. Revoking/regenerating a link requires updating subscribers.

iCal is not an instant channel manager. It does not exchange full prices, payments or every reservation detail and cannot guarantee no double bookings. Confirm changes in the OTA's own calendar too.
07 · Your current retention decision

Photos and the 45-day policy

Current setting: OFF for every property. The admin chooses when to enable deletion for each property. This handbook does not enable it.

Which photos can expire?

Only eligible before/after images in completed cleaning logs, stored solely in R2. Each needs 45 full days from its own upload. A later photo gets its own 45 days, even in an older task. There is no third-cleaning-set requirement.

UploadThe photo's own clock starts.
Days 1–44Protected from this cleanup.
45 days or laterEligible only if that property's deletion is on and safeguards pass.

Turn deletion on or off

  1. Open Notices → Security, storage & backups → Cleaning photo retention.
  2. Review the property, status and eligible count.
  3. Enter a reason.
  4. Select Turn on deletion for [property] and confirm the notice; or select Turn off deletion for [property] to pause.

Enabling can make already-old eligible photos removable at the next run. Turning it off stops future removal for that property, including pending recovery-copy removal; it cannot restore deleted files. Other properties are unaffected.

Outside this rule

  • Property covers.
  • Recorded damage/incident evidence and protected/shared photos.
  • Owner-report and training attachments.
  • Invoice, expense, booking and payout records.
  • Guest IDs, videos and unbound uploads.
  • Cleaning history, dates, notes, checklists and approvals.

Keep evidence for review

Admin → cleaning record → admin options → note → Keep photos for damage or review.

A hold protects that cleaning's evidence. Release it only when appropriate. The app cannot identify unlabelled damage from an image: record it or place a hold.

Active/reopened tasks, unknown upload dates and retained migration copies stay protected.

Expiry is permanent and includes the photo's encrypted recovery copy after verification. There is no extra seven-day recovery promise under this policy. A placeholder remains in the cleaning record; failed removal is retried safely.

Existing MongoDB photos: leave untouched. You chose to handle them manually later. Photos with retained MongoDB rollback copies are excluded from automatic deletion. This release does not remove those files or business collections.

Cleanup checks hourly while the backend is awake. It can run later than the 45-day threshold, never earlier. Holds and paused cleanup use more storage.

Taking and uploading pictures

Use Take photo or Choose from library. Ordinary uploads accept JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF, BMP, TIFF and GIF. The first frame/page is used where applicable. The app corrects orientation and compresses photos. A 50-megapixel image limit and 100 MB ordinary-upload limit apply; guest-ID limits are separate. Megapixels measure width × height, not file size.

Use clear, well-lit evidence and wait for saving. A pending photo can be retried after a network failure. After a browser reload you may need to discard an unavailable draft and select the file again.

A branch is a code version; a commit is a saved change. A pull request records review. GitHub Actions runs checks. A deployment starts a chosen version on Render.

Saving to GitHub does not instantly update the app. Automatic deployment is off. An authorised maintainer manually deploys the tested release. The current live branch is deploy/render-free; do not assume the default branch contains the latest app.

For each update

  1. Describe the issue and preserve real records.
  2. Make changes in a separate branch and test with synthetic data.
  3. Review the pull request and relevant checks.
  4. Advance the live branch to the approved commit.
  5. In the existing Render service: Manual Deploy → Deploy latest commit.
  6. Wait for Live, then check health, sign-in, access and the affected workflow.
  7. Record the release and any refresh/sign-in steps for users.

Code rollback does not automatically reverse data edits, expired photos or external actions. A branch does not isolate data either: staging needs separate records, media and secrets.

10 · Protecting business records

Backups and security

What the backup does

Business records are exported and encrypted before being stored in the private backup bucket. Ordinary photos have separate encrypted recovery files. A run is verified only after cloud read-back, decryption and integrity checks.

  • The schedule aims for about 02:00 IST daily, with overdue catch-up while running.
  • It keeps seven verified database snapshots.
  • Admin → Notices → Security, storage & backups → Back up now queues a run. Wait, then Refresh service status.
  • A timestamp ending +00:00 is UTC; India is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead.
  • The cleaning expiry rule removes that expired photo's recovery copy too. Protected covers and other media do not inherit that rule.
A verified backup is not a completed restore drill. An actual cloud snapshot and representative media still need restoring into isolated targets without overwriting live data. Free-service sleep can delay jobs; reliable fixed-time scheduling and external alerts remain follow-up work.

Already implemented

  • Backend role/property access checks.
  • HTTPS, protected browser sessions and unsafe-request restrictions.
  • Private files served through short-lived file-specific tickets.
  • Upload validation, size limits and login throttling.
  • Separate media/backup credentials.
  • Encrypted backups and integrity checks.
  • Optional admin authenticator and recovery codes.
  • Account deactivation, session controls and supported admin-action audits.

What admins must do

Enroll your authenticator from the security page and save recovery codes privately. The last checked admin account had not enrolled. Protect GitHub, Render, Atlas and Cloudflare accounts with their own two-step verification and unique passwords.

Keep storage keys, database credentials, the app signing secret and especially the backup encryption key in a secure private password manager. Losing the encryption key can make backups unusable. Do not paste secrets into chat, GitHub or the handbook. Role login passwords are not included here.

Share report links only with intended people, sign out on shared devices and deactivate accounts when staff leave. Turning photo deletion off cannot restore already-expired files.

11 · Operations and scaling

Keeping the app running and growing

Business checks

  • Daily: arrivals, departures, cleaning and notices.
  • After important changes: refresh and check the saved record.
  • Before payouts: check statements, expenses and held evidence.
  • Before inviting someone: check role, property and task assignment.

Admin/maintainer checks

  • Review backup freshness and unresolved notices.
  • Review storage/usage and account access.
  • Keep private recovery information current.
  • Schedule restore drills and security/dependency reviews.

Current app storage budgets

Conservative budgets are approximately 3 GiB for live media and 4 GiB for backups, with operation limits and separate test headroom. These are app safeguards, not provider-enforced zero-cost guarantees; other account activity can consume allowances. If a guard stops an upload, investigate usage or approve capacity rather than removing protection blindly.

For around 40 users

Measure busy-hour activity, simultaneous uploads, file sizes, response times, errors, CPU/memory and database/media growth. Forty registered users differs from forty simultaneous large uploads. Concurrent role tests check access separation; they are not a benchmark of the free server.

  1. Dependable scheduling: review always-on compute and external health/backup alerts before relying on uninterrupted operations.
  2. Database: choose capacity based on measured use and the approved backup/restore plan.
  3. Media: review holds, retention choices and allowances before enlarging budgets.
  4. Separate testing: create isolated cloud staging with its own files, records and credentials.
  5. More servers: load-test workers/replicas, uploads and jobs before increasing concurrency.

No paid upgrade was enabled for this release. Verify current pricing, tax, currency conversion and account usage before purchasing. Paying for compute alone does not complete security, backup recovery or operational readiness.
